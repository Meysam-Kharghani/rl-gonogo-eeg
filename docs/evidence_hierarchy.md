# Evidence hierarchy — EEG-computational study

## 1. Cue-window action prediction
Combined nominal cue-window EEG does not provide a detectable held-out action-prediction increment beyond the selected behavioral/computational reference.

## 2. Cue-window RL-state encoding
Combined cue-window EEG does not improve held-out prediction of cross-fitted policy probability, actor-value difference, or control beyond task design and lagged behavioral history.

## 3. Stability of the cue null
Repeated outer partitions preserve the negative or near-null cue increment across all tested seeds. A richer lagged behavioral-history comparator also preserves the cue-null conclusion.

## 4. Response-locked performance monitoring
The fixed response-signature family shows strong false-alarm-minus-Go-hit contrasts. Response-time matching preserves these differences, but within-NoGo correct-rejection versus false-alarm comparisons and target × response interactions do not provide multiplicity-corrected evidence for pure error specificity.

**Interpretation:** these are performance-monitoring contrasts, not pure error-specific effects.

## 5. Reliability
Split-half reliability is heterogeneous across response endpoints. Associations involving lower-reliability signatures require correspondingly cautious interpretation.

## 6. Between-participant RL–EEG associations
The corrected fixed 18-test family provides supportive **between-participant covariation**. An expanded 33-test family retains a smaller robust core. The trial-level cross-fitted RL–EEG analysis is null after multiplicity correction and grouped prediction is near zero.

**Interpretation:** the evidence does not establish trial-wise neural implementation, causal mapping, or a one-to-one physiological realization of the fitted RL states.

## 7. Temporal support
Cue analyses use nominal cue-window features. Zero-phase/non-causal filtering means they cannot establish strictly prospective temporal information. This limitation remains even though subject-level cross-fitting prevents model-fitting leakage.
