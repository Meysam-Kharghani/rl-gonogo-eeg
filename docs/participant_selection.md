# Participant selection

The computational sample contains 40 participants: 20 high-risk and 20 low-risk.

Selection followed the sampling logic reported for the original study: adjusted Balloon Analogue Risk
Task (BART) scores defined the top 20% of the score distribution as the high-risk stratum and the
bottom 20% as the low-risk stratum, with random selection from those extreme strata.

Some participants in the present computational sample overlap with participants reported in the
earlier study, while other participants were recorded within the same research program but were not
included in that earlier report. The present work does not define or analyze a privileged
"original-32" subset.

The final sample comprised 36 right-handed and four left-handed participants.

No participant identifiers are distributed in the public repository.

Reference: Nazari MA et al. (2024), *Brain and Cognition* 175, 106136,
doi:10.1016/j.bandc.2024.106136.
